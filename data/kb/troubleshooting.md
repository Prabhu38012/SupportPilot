# Troubleshooting and Limits

## Notifications are not arriving
Check Profile > Notifications to confirm email and push are enabled for the project. For email, look in spam and add notifications@acme-tasks.example to your contacts. For mobile push, make sure notifications are allowed for the Acme Tasks app in your phone settings.

## Supported browsers and apps
Acme Tasks works on the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. The iOS app requires iOS 15 or later. The Android app requires Android 10 or later; Android 9 and older are not supported.

## File upload limits
The maximum file size is 25 MB on the Free plan and 100 MB on Pro and Business. Each workspace includes 5 GB of storage on Free and 100 GB per member on paid plans.

## Exporting your data
Owners and Admins can export all workspace data as CSV or JSON under Settings > Export. Large exports are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed within 30 minutes.

## The app is slow or not loading
Check the status page at status.acme-tasks.example. Then try a hard refresh, disable browser extensions and test in a private window. If it still fails, include a screenshot of the browser console when contacting support.
