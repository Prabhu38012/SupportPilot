# Security and Compliance

## Encryption
All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256.

## Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on is available on the Business plan and works with Okta, Azure AD and Google Workspace. SSO is not available on Free or Pro.

## Data residency
Workspaces are hosted in either the US or the EU region. The region is chosen when the workspace is created and cannot be changed afterwards.

## Compliance
Acme Tasks is SOC 2 Type II audited. A copy of the latest report is available to Business customers on request through their account manager.

## Audit logs
Audit logs record sign-ins, permission changes and exports. They are available on the Business plan and retained for 12 months.
