# Billing and Plans

## Plans and pricing
Acme Tasks has three plans. Free supports up to 5 members and 3 projects. Pro costs $8 per member per month ($80 per member per year when billed annually). Business costs $15 per member per month and adds SAML single sign-on, audit logs and priority support.

## Changing your plan
Workspace owners can change plans in Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep paid features until then.

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact after every payment. You can download past invoices as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices. To change where invoices are sent, edit the billing contact under Settings > Billing > Billing contact.

## Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months, but you can cancel any time to stop future charges.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email the billing contact. If all retries fail the workspace becomes read-only. Update your card under Settings > Billing > Payment method to restore access.

## Cancelling your subscription
Owners can cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Your workspace data is kept for 30 days after cancellation and can be restored by resubscribing within that time.
