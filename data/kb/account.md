# Account and Workspace

## Reset your password
On the sign-in page choose "Forgot password" and enter your email. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check spam and confirm you used the email on the account.

## Change your email address
Go to Profile > Account > Email, enter the new address and confirm using the link sent to it. Your old address stops working for sign-in after confirmation.

## Two-factor authentication
Enable two-factor authentication under Profile > Security using any authenticator app. Save the 10 recovery codes shown during setup. Owners on the Business plan can require 2FA for all members.

## Inviting members and guests
Admins and owners invite people under Members > Invite. Members get access to all workspace projects they are added to. Guests are free, can be added to individual projects only, and cannot see other projects or workspace settings.

## Roles and permissions
There are four roles: Owner, Admin, Member and Guest. Only Owners can manage billing, transfer ownership or delete the workspace. Admins manage members and projects.

## Deleting your workspace
Only the Owner can delete a workspace under Settings > General > Delete workspace. Deletion is permanent after 30 days, and during those 30 days the Owner can restore it from the same page.
